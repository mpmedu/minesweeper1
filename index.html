Rules

1. The minefield is a grid of 100 squares. There are landmines on 20 of the squares. To win you must defuse all the mines. If you step on a mine, ie left-click on a square with a mine, then the game is over. To defuse the mines you must right-click on the squares where the mines are. You are shielded when right-clicking but you are only given 5 chances to right-click.

2. If you right-click on a mine then the mine is defused and you don't lose a chance - you only lose a chance when right-clicking on a square that does not contain a mine.

3.If you left-click on a square not containing a mine then that square is marked as checked/cleared. If the square is next to one or more mines then the number of mines that surround that square is displayed in the square. If you left-click on a square which is not next to a mine, ie an empty square, then its surrounding squares are checked - this is a recursive procedure meaning that whenever an empty square is found its surrounding squares will also be checked.
